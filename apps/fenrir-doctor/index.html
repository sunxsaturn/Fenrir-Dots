AUTOMATIC DISCOVERY
Scheduled campaign queue

              
Select one or more sources and visual themes. Fenrir creates a finite queue of unique combinations and rotates through them sequentially.

Up to 18 unique source/profile campaigns can be queued. Each campaign advances in bounded, resumable metadata-only slices; a completed campaign advances to the next queued combination. The finite queue stops for human review when exhausted, or stops safely on provider/catalog errors. Paused queues can be resumed without repeating completed campaigns. No wallpaper files are downloaded, licensed, accepted or synchronized by this scheduler.
